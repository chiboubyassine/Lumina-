Art and Artist

After my studies, I felt the need to create something that truly reflects who I am — a business that combines my passion with a way to make a living. This is how I built a venture that brings my artistic vision to life. Each candle is carefully crafted with natural ingredients and infused with creativity to bring light and warmth to your special moments. Lumina represents more than just candles; it's a celebration of art, craftsmanship, and the beauty of light. Every piece tells a story and carries a part of my journey as an artist and entrepreneur.
